# GEO Optimizer Lite — Claude Skill

Free AI search optimization audit tool for Claude. Score your content's citability across Perplexity, ChatGPT Browse, Google AI Overviews, and Bing Copilot.

## What It Does

Audit any URL or content block across 4 AI-citability dimensions:

1. **Direct Answer Density** — Does your content have extractable facts AI can quote?
2. **Query-Intent Alignment** — Does your page answer the query in the first 100 words?
3. **Freshness Signals** — Is your content visibly current and recently updated?
4. **Conversation-Ready Format** — Can AI easily reformulate your content into answers?

Get a score out of 40, priority fixes, and 3 quick wins per page.

## Install

1. Download SKILL.md from this repo
2. Place it in your Claude skills directory
3. Ask Claude: "Audit this page for AI search citability: [URL]"
